# Guardrails

- Use only data the operator is authorized to process.
- Do not place passwords, session cookies, private keys or raw access tokens in inputs.
- Do not bypass robots controls, paywalls, authentication, rate limits or platform restrictions.
- Require explicit human approval for external mutations and high-impact decisions.
- Preserve evidence, provenance, exclusions and uncertainty.
- Fail closed when authorization or required evidence is missing.
